Each score cell represents one suite and one perturbation direction. Overall, mode, and suite scores are arithmetic means of the available S01–S21 and D01–D21 cells. D19 has no available results; Spatial S04 is also unavailable, leaving 83 static and 80 dynamic cells.

OpenVLA-OFT_w, OpenVLA-OFT_m, OpenVLA-OFT+, and RIPT-VLA use completed evaluations of eight tasks (two per suite), with 30 rollouts per task and case. Successes and episodes are pooled within each suite and direction before averaging cells. The linked counts also retain S22, which is excluded from this ranking. Other model scores retain the October 1 source-sheet snapshot.

Base SR is the mean of the four suite BASE scores, shown only when all are available. Robustness Drop is Base SR minus Perturbed SR. Empty and N/A values are omitted, never converted to zero.
